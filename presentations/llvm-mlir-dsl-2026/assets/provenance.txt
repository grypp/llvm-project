MLIR DSL tutorial presentation assets
=====================================

Template
--------
template.pptx is a one-slide extraction of llvm-mlir-dsl-tutorial-v3.pptx.
Only the editable Logo Eye and Logo Text shapes remain on its slide. The
original slide master, layouts, themes, and all five embedded font parts
are retained. The source tutorial used the user-supplied presentation
cutlass-python-pytorch-v22.pptx (PyTorch Conference 2026) as its template.
No unrelated source-presentation slides are included in this template.
Canvas: 10 x 5.625 inches. Template and logos retain their original rights.

Fonts
-----
NVIDIASans_Rg.ttf, NVIDIASans_Bd.ttf, and NVIDIASans_Lt.ttf are unchanged
copies of the existing NVIDIA Sans installation used to build the tutorial.
The files retain their original font metadata and rights; bundling them
here does not relicense them under the LLVM source-code license. The
embedded NVIDIA font parts retain editable embedding permissions (fsType=8).
No separate NVIDIA font license file was present with that installation.

JetBrainsMono-Regular.ttf and JetBrainsMono-Bold.ttf are unchanged copies
of the reference presentation's JetBrains Mono 2.304 assets. Their license
is fonts/JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
Upstream: https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono/releases/tag/v2.304
The embedded JetBrains Mono font parts retain their original installable
embedding permissions (fsType=0).
